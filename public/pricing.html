Common questions
Does this replace my coach?
No — and it is not trying to. Forty Fifteen surfaces patterns and gives you structured observations. A good coach applies real-time judgment. This gives you and your coach better information to work with.
How accurate is the analysis?
The engine identifies recurring technical and tactical patterns with strong accuracy on good quality video. Uncertain observations are labelled as "possible" rather than claiming false precision. Best results come from 10–20 minute videos filmed side-on at 720p or higher.
Can I use it for lessons and drills?
Yes. Forty Fifteen has three modes — Match, Drills, and Lesson. The entire report adapts to what you select. Lesson mode focuses entirely on technique. Drills mode goes deep on the specific shot being practiced.
What happens to my video?
Your video is processed to extract frames and then discarded. We do not store your footage. Frames are analyzed and deleted once your report is generated.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. No fees, no questions. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
